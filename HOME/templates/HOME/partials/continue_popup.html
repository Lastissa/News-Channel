{% load static %} {% comment %} CONTINUE READING POPUP (near full-screen sheet). Shown on the home page (HOME/home.html) and, for signed in readers only, on a story page (blog/story_detail.html), through the base template's "continue_popup" block. It opens a few seconds after the page loads, then stays quiet for a cooldown that depends on the page: 12 hours on the home page and 1 hour on a story page (continue-popup.js, data-continue-scope / data-continue-cooldown). The sheet is full width and touches the bottom on phones; on tablet/desktop (768px and up) it becomes a centered panel with the blurred page dimmed around it (continue-popup.css). Tapping the backdrop, the close button, or pressing Escape dismisses it. Layout follows the Sahara Reporters / Taboola "Keep on reading" overlay: a slim header, then a dense magazine mix -- a horizontally scrolling rail plus a grid whose every seventh card becomes a full-width horizontal banner, the rest are vertical thumbnail cards. Guests get a single compact "sign in" bar, not a full-height prompt. Signed in readers get their last reads rail and a feed built from those stories' categories. Page 1 is rendered here; the script asks home:continue_feed for page 2+ as the reader scrolls, with a skeleton loader. {% endcomment %}
