Formatting guide

Review checks and formatting codes for your story.

The review checks decide whether your story will be accepted. The formatting codes are optional, but they split the article into sections and help our online visibility. Plain paragraphs works but they are not advisable.

Check today’s news first

  1. Before you write, open abureport.com.ng and check today’s news. If we have not published that news yet, go ahead and write it. If we have already published it, do not publish it again.

Edit rules

  1. The heading cannot be changed once a story is published, because the story link is built from it. Only the image, category and content can be edited.
  2. Re-review your content and make sure it aligns with {{ project_name }} guidelines before saving.
  3. Keep the content SEO friendly: a clear headline, an opening paragraph that states what happened, with facts ,who, where and/or when and if necessary sections with headings.
  4. Avoid frequent repetition of content in the article. Say each fact once.
  5. Avoid claims we cannot independently verify while writing news.
