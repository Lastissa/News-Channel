Formatting guide

Review checks and formatting codes for your story.

The review checks decide whether your story is accepted. The formatting codes are optional, but they split the article into sections and help search engines index it. Plain paragraphs work, but they are not advisable.

Review before you publish

  1. Re-review your content and make sure it aligns with {{ project_name }} guidelines before publishing.
  2. The first paragraph can have at most 70% similarity with the heading. Open with new information, not a copy of the headline.
  3. Provide an image description for the headline image. It is shown beneath the image.
  4. Keep the content SEO friendly: a clear headline, an opening paragraph that states what happened, who, where and when, and short sections with headings.
  5. Avoid frequent repetition of content in the article. Say each fact once.

Rules checked when you publish

  1. Do not begin the content with a # heading that repeats the headline. The headline already appears above the story.
  2. The story needs real content, not just a heading or filler. A very short story may trigger a warning; you can edit it or confirm that it contains enough information to post.
  3. Use headings for real sections. Empty ones like # or ## are rejected.
  4. Do not use lines of symbols such as ------ or ====== or ******. Leave a blank line instead.
  5. Start with a normal paragraph where possible. Markdown headings are allowed for real sections, but do not repeat the main headline at the start of the story.
