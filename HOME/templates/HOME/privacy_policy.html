Cookies and local storage

We may use cookies or browser storage to keep you signed in, maintain a secure form experience, and remember your chosen theme. We use this information only to improve usability and site functionality. You may disable cookies from your browser settings, but some features may not work as intended.

For authentication-related pages, we may also use short-lived security cookies and rate-limiting controls to detect repeated rapid page refreshes or automated traffic. This helps protect our sign-in and sign-up endpoints from abuse, reduces unnecessary server load, and helps us verify that real users are interacting with the page. When a pattern of excessive refreshes is detected, we may temporarily limit access for a short period and ask the user to wait a few seconds before retrying.

Bookmarks, reading history and saved content

When you save stories or bookmark content, we may record that activity against your account so you can revisit it later. This information is kept to offer a better user experience and is not sold to third parties AND NEVER WILL IT BE USED.
