What information do we collect?

We may collect personal information such as your email address when you create an account, subscribe to our newsletter, save content, or contact us.

We also collect non-personal information such as browser type, device information, referring pages and basic usage data. This is used to improve performance and understand how readers use our content, and only where it is genuinely needed.

Personal information is collected only when you volunteer it, and you may choose not to provide it. In some cases, declining may limit access to certain features.

How do we use collected information?

We use personal information to run the site, keep your account secure, personalise your experience, send important updates and respond to enquiries. Aggregated data helps us measure content performance and improve our stories and features.

If you opt in to the newsletter, we may send updates on the topics you selected. You can unsubscribe at any time by signing in and turning the newsletter off on your profile page, or by contacting our team through the site. If you joined through the newsletter form and never set a password, contact us and we will help you unsubscribe.

Cookies and local storage

We may use cookies or browser storage to keep you signed in, secure our forms and remember your chosen theme. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may stop working as intended.

On sign-in and sign-up pages we may also use short-lived security cookies and rate limiting to detect rapid repeated refreshes or automated traffic. This protects those pages from abuse and reduces unnecessary server load. If excessive refreshing is detected, we may briefly limit access and ask you to wait a few seconds before trying again.

Bookmarks, reading history and saved content

When you save or bookmark stories, we record that activity against your account so you can come back to it later. This information exists only to give you a better experience. It is not sold to third parties, and it will never be used for any other purpose.

How do we protect your information?

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards to protect the personal information we store. No method of transmission or storage is completely risk-free, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Do we disclose information to third parties?

We do not sell, trade or rent personal information to outside parties. In limited cases we share information with trusted service providers who help us run the website or manage communication, such as email tools or technical support, and only for the agreed purpose.

We may also disclose information if required by law or court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and platform.

Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party sites, including educational resources, institutions and advertisers. These sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content, practices or handling of data.

Advertising and analytics

We may work with advertising or analytics partners to understand how visitors use the site. These services may use cookies or similar tools to collect non-personal usage data. This policy does not cover third-party advertising or tracking beyond our direct platform controls.

Data retention and your rights

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. You may ask to access, update, export or delete your account information, and you can ask for your newsletter subscription to be removed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the date at the top of this page will change, and significant changes will be noted in the content.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, use the contact details in the footer of this website or send an enquiry through the contact page.
