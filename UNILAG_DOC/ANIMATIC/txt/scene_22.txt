Today, walk the ground and count the proof. Twelve faculties. Three campuses, Akoka, Yaba, Idi-Araba. Thirteen Vice-Chancellors since Njoku, and the thirteenth, Professor Folasade Ogunsola, the first woman to hold the office. Enterprise has its own wings: UNILAG Consult, UNILAG Ventures. Research kept reaching, too. In later years, the record does not give the exact date, a Hydraulic Research Laboratory rose, with support from the Government of the Netherlands and thirty thousand naira from the Nigerian Ports Authority.
