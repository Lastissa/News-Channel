## STEP 2 — LOGLINE, THEMES, CHAPTER TABLE

**LOGLINE**
On 22 October 1962, a newborn nation answered its own hunger with an Act of Parliament — and from borrowed rooms in Surulere, the University of Lagos rose beside the lagoon to become, in deed and in truth, the nation's pride.

**THREE THEMES**
1. **A nation builds itself by building its people** — the hunger for professionals, from Yaba Higher College to the Ashby report to Act No. 11.
2. **Law before stones** — the Act, the Council, the twin institutions, the motto as contract; and the 2012 test of the name, which proved the identity was worth defending.
3. **Light as promise kept** — the lamp of the first morning, the Lagoon Lighthouse, dawn over the lagoon; the motto "In deed and in truth" as a filament running through all of it.

**DESCRIPTION**
On 22 October 1962 — three weeks after Nigeria's second Independence Day — about 130 students heard the first lectures of a brand-new university, in borrowed rooms in Surulere, Lagos. This film tells the birth of the University of Lagos as it happened: the hunger of a new nation for trained professionals, the Act of Parliament that created it, the botanist who became its first Vice-Chancellor, the campus that rose at Akoka, its growth into three campuses and twelve faculties — and the 2012 test of its very name, told with fairness to everyone involved.

Every claim is labelled on screen: FACT, CONTEXT-VERIFY, or RECONSTRUCTION. No AI likenesses of real people. AI-generated frames are always marked "Illustrative image."

Sources: UNILAG's official history; Akinyele & Adeboye, "A History of the University of Lagos, 1962–2012"; National Archives; Nigerian newspaper archives.
